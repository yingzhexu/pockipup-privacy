Local Features Available Now

The app includes virtual pet interactions, prewritten local conversations, map exploration, mini-games, a virtual shop, inventory and collections, farming, travel keepsakes, story reading, plans, and countdowns.

The map is an illustrated world installed with the app, not a location map. Park friends and town characters are software characters, not online players or real contacts. The shop uses in-game coins. There are currently no real-money payments, ticket purchases, in-app purchases, or cash redemption of virtual items. Conversations also use in-game coins: the first chat on a topic costs 25 coins and chatting again costs 2. Coins are tracked only on this device, have no real-world value, and cannot be purchased or redeemed.

The story is original in-app content installed with the app. By default, story interactions must be completed before the reading continues; you can turn on “Skip Story Moments” in Settings. Readers' choices create local reading memories and do not rewrite the story's fixed content. “Reading Impressions” are literary expressions, not personality tests, psychological assessments, or real-world credit judgments. Completing Volume One ends at “To be continued.”

Default Name and Your Chosen Nickname

The virtual puppy’s default name is Huqing, shown as “Huqing” in the English interface. The first-launch welcome screen includes an optional nickname field. Keeping the default or leaving it blank uses the default name. You can also change your puppy’s nickname in My Pup. This is a pet nickname, not an online account username, and you do not need to enter your real name.

Nicknames accept Chinese characters or English letters: up to four Chinese characters or six English letters. Mixed names use the same length validation as the app. After a change, supported interface text, written dialogue, story presentation, and this app’s system reminder presentation use your chosen nickname. Reminder titles and notes you enter stay as written. Renaming does not erase pet, reading, or travel progress, rewrite the fixed story content, or regenerate prerecorded character voices or story recordings. Existing audio may still include the original recorded name. The English interface continues to use the existing Chinese recordings.

Nickname and language preferences stay on your device. The app shares these presentation settings locally with its widget and Live Activity extension through its own App Group; it does not upload them to a developer server. Your nickname may appear on the Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, or supported companion displays.

This document’s explanation of “Huqing,” its name order, creative background, and namesake statements describes the default fictional character. It does not assign that name structure to a nickname you choose or imply participation or permission from a real person with the same name.

To restore your experience, the app saves the following in its local app sandbox and preferences:

  • Pet status, bond level, interaction counts, conversation topics and progress, and saved lines;
  • Map progress, in-game coins, experience, item quantities, decorations, home setup, fields and harvest times, quests and daily reward records, virtual friends, collections, travel stamps, and travel notes;
  • Dates, fictional locations, pet expressions, and lighting choices for travel keepsakes;
  • Story chapter and paragraph position, furthest reading progress, completed chapters, interactive events, memories, footprints, reader choices and their timestamps, bookmarks and their timestamps, text size, font, dark reading, and interaction-display preferences;
  • User-created plan titles, notes, types, due dates, creation dates, completion states, reminder preferences, and local identifiers and processing states needed to maintain notifications, Live Activities, and system timers;

This data supports local features and progress restoration; it is not uploaded to a developer server. Reading choices are not used for advertising profiles, and interaction or quest records are not used to profile real people. The app has no online account, advertising, cross-app tracking, developer-operated remote analytics, or online generative AI requests. Because you enter plan titles and notes, please avoid adding identity documents, health or financial details, or other people's private information unrelated to the reminder.

Travel Keepsakes and Device Permissions

Travel photos are in-app keepsakes composed from built-in map scenes, pet expressions, and lighting choices. They currently do not use the camera, record audio, read the system photo library, or save to it. The app stores local card options and timestamps rather than photographs of real scenes. It keeps up to the 30 most recent keepsakes and 40 most recent travel notes.

Story poster: after finishing Volume One, when you tap “Make a Poster and Save to Photos” in “This Volume's Memories,” the app creates an image on this device and requests only the iOS “Add Photos” permission to save it to the system photo library. The app does not read or upload any photos in your library, and declining does not affect other features. Once saved, the poster is managed by you and the system photo library.

Plan Reminders and Three System Controls

Standard notifications: The first time you open Plans, you can choose Full Notifications or Dynamic Island Only. Choosing full notifications or turning on system notifications requests iOS notification authorization when needed. If permission was previously denied, you must change it in Settings. Dynamic Island Only does not request standard notification permission. Standard reminders are scheduled locally and can be disabled in the app or system settings.

Live Activities: Enabled in the app by default; whether they appear still depends on iOS Live Activity settings, the device, and system state. They may show a plan title, part of its notes, and a countdown on the Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, and supported related system surfaces. They are separate from standard notification permission. Local ActivityKit provides them. The app does not register a push token with a developer server or rely on developer-operated remote pushes. The system may limit their number, duration, display, and alerts; permanent display is not promised.

Optional System Clock Mode: Off by default. Only turning it on requests separate alarm and timer authorization, on systems that support AlarmKit. After authorization, some plans use system alarms or timers, with pause, resume, and snooze controls. This authorization is separate from standard notifications. System alarms may alert even in Silent or Focus mode. Turning this mode off attempts to cancel timers it manages and restore applicable standard plan reminders.

System components process plans locally. Titles and notes may be visible to people who can see your Lock Screen or linked devices. You can edit plans, disable the relevant features, or adjust notification previews and permissions in system settings. System and linked-device behavior follows those providers' rules.

What Reminders Can and Cannot Guarantee

Plan reminders are for everyday scheduling. They are not emergency, medical, life-safety, or other services that must guarantee on-time delivery. Notification settings, Focus, scheduled summaries, a powered-off device, battery state, system version, Live Activity limits, and changes to device time may affect display or alerts.

A standard Live Activity shows a due state when its countdown ends. Opening the app clears the relevant due card, while unfinished plans remain in the list. Ending or dismissing a card does not complete the plan. System Clock Mode follows Apple's system timer and alarm behavior. The current app does not guarantee that every Lock Screen or Dynamic Island card will disappear automatically after a specified number of minutes.

Audio, Silent Mode, and Story Interactions

The app includes background music, an opening soundtrack, interface and game feedback, puppy dialogue, story interaction sounds, and the dream recording. These are bundled local audio files. Playback does not require microphone access and does not record, identify, or upload your voice. Dialogue uses scripted text and prerecorded audio, not an online AI chat service.

Background music follows the app’s Silent Mode logic, and the opening soundtrack fades in gradually. Puppy dialogue, button sounds, and story audio use a media playback audio session and may remain audible in Ring/Silent mode. System volume, your audio output device, audio interruptions, and the relevant in-app switches still affect playback. Audio settings let you adjust music, character voices, and effects separately, turn off automatic puppy voice playback, and disable story interaction sounds or the dream recording’s audio. Delete Data and Start Over

After a second confirmation, My Pet → Settings → Delete Data & Start Over attempts to stop audio, end this app's Live Activities, cancel its pending and delivered notifications, cancel system alarms and timers it manages, and clear persistent app preferences. This includes pet and chat progress, the map, inventory, coins, fields, quests, travel keepsakes, plans, story reading position, choices, bookmarks, collections, reading preferences, and agreement acceptance records. On success, the app returns to the welcome screen. Continuing requires you to actively accept the documents again.

Deleting progress cannot retract content already heard or displayed, and does not delete bundled images, story text, voice clips, or other app resources. If permissions or system components prevent reminders from being canceled, check their actual system state. If deletion fails, follow the app's message.

This operation does not clear iOS notification, Live Activity, or alarm permission decisions. Accepting the documents again is not a new system authorization. Manage those permissions separately in Settings. The app has no developer-hosted cloud saves or independent progress import, export, or recovery service. System backups, device migration, and linked-device records follow system rules. In-app deletion does not guarantee deletion of those system copies.

Days to Go and Important Dates

Version 3.5 adds Days to Go within Plans. You can save birthdays, anniversaries, trips, goals, and other dates; count calendar days ahead or behind; categorize, pin, search, sort, repeat yearly or monthly, edit, and delete them. Dates are saved as Gregorian year, month, and day and counted using calendar days in the device’s current time zone, with today as day 0. February 29 uses February 28 in non-leap years; shorter months use their last day.

IDs, names, notes, dates, categories, repeat rules, pin status, reminder preferences, reminder times, advance-reminder days, and creation times stay on this device. The feature does not read or write system calendars, contacts, birthday records, or Reminders and does not connect to the internet. Respect other people’s privacy when entering their birthdays or anniversaries. Using this feature does not require submitting identification or contact details.

You may choose a reminder on the day and an additional reminder 1, 3, or 7 days before. Date reminders reuse the app’s existing system notification preference and permission. Without notification permission, dates can still be saved and shown in widgets. The feature does not automatically request calendar, contacts, or extra Live Activity permission, and does not turn every distant date into an ongoing Live Activity. It currently does not provide long-term Days to Go countdowns in Dynamic Island.

Repeating reminders use a bounded rolling queue: local notifications are scheduled for the next occurrence and up to three later occurrences. When system notification slots are limited, the nearest reminders take priority. Later reminders are replenished when you reopen the app. Already scheduled notifications can be delivered while the app is closed, but unscheduled future reminders cannot be guaranteed if you do not open the app for a long time. Reopening reschedules them for the device’s current time zone. If you change time zones without reopening, previously scheduled reminders follow their previous schedule. Silent mode, Focus, permission, and notification settings can affect their appearance or sound.

The Days to Go and Important Days widgets share limited, read-only date-display data: IDs, titles, dates, categories, repeat rules, and pin status. They do not share date notes or reminder settings. Widget configuration lets you choose an event or automatically follow pinned and nearby days, and select clear glass or soft cream. The actual transparent Home Screen glass follows system clear or tinted appearance settings. The app cannot force a system appearance or read your wallpaper. The title visibility switch applies to both plans and important days. iOS schedules date updates, which may be delayed. Deleting a day cancels its pending reminders and updates its display copy. Delete All Data also clears these saves.
